About

I’m a data analyst who enjoys the moment a messy table turns into a clear answer. My projects start with a business question: where the profit really comes from, why orders are falling, which customers are likely to leave. I then build the model, the dashboard and the recommendation around it.

I’m careful about what data can and can’t prove, so my write-ups state their limits next to their findings. I’m looking for a data analyst role where I can keep learning, and I also take on freelance dashboard and reporting work.
